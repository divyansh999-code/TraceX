## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rigor, discretion, and exacting authority of an institutional intelligence briefing combined with the real-time operational posture of a modern forensic laboratory. It is engineered specifically for national security researchers, corporate intelligence units, and geopolitical analysts tasked with deciphering narrative warfare, coordinated influence campaigns, and emerging volatility across unstructured communication channels.

The design movement balances **Tactile Technical Minimalism** with **Editorial Precision**:
- **Utilitarian Discipline:** Reject ornamental flourishes, decorative glow effects, faux dimensional gradients, and speculative "sci-fi" tropes. The interface presents as a precision-calibrated workstation, delivering extreme signal-to-noise ratio where every pixel serves cognitive triage.
- **Archival & Editorial Gravitas:** Warm editorial typography balances clinical data displays, giving threat reports and narrative assessments the gravity of high-level diplomatic cables or academic intelligence syntheses.
- **Restrained Urgency:** Information hierarchy is maintained through flat high-contrast signifiers, micro-hairlines, and calibrated semantic pigments rather than excessive physical elevation or flashing animations.

## Colors

The palette enforces a dedicated, low-eye-strain nocturnal monitoring environment. Contrast is achieved via value shifts and hairline delineation rather than luminous saturation.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0A0E14`):** Deep ink navy-black ground establishing the lowest baseline depth.
- **Panel Surface (`#12161F`):** Primary working tier for docked consoles, inspection panels, and workspace grids.
- **Elevated Surface (`#171C27`):** Focused modules, active states, split-pane headers, and floating inspectors.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#232838`):** Strict 1px structural framing separating high-density views without heavy shadow occlusion.

### Typography & Content Tokens
- **Primary Text (`#EDECE6`):** Warm off-white, providing organic legibility without the harsh ocular fatigue of cold `#FFFFFF`.
- **Secondary / Muted Text (`#8B92A5`):** Slate gray-blue for secondary metadata, standard labels, and supportive analysis.
- **Tertiary / Disabled Text (`#565C6B`):** Muted carbon for inactive icons, timestamp brackets, and structural guides.

### Accents & Signal Semantics
- **Brand Accent (`#E8823A`):** Burnt amber / signal orange. Applied flat for selected targets, active filters, scrub-head playheads, and narrative focus indicators. Never deployed as a gradient.
- **Verified / Low Risk (`#3EAF7C`):** Muted sage green signifying verified human origin, authentic source provenance, and benign content flow.
- **Warning / Medium Risk (`#D9A441`):** Muted gold signaling anomalous amplification velocity, coordinated bot patterns, or unverified claims.
- **Alert / High Risk (`#D9564F`):** Muted brick red indicating confirmed dis-information vectors, active doxxing/brigading, or critical destabilization narratives.
- **Neutral Data (`#5FA1C4`):** Dusty cyan used strictly for telemetry markers, channel metadata, baseline telemetry, and non-evaluative frequency graphs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements a three-family triage protocol:
1. **Newsreader (Display/Headlines):** Imparts authoritative analytical cadence. Used for narrative executive summaries, high-level briefing titles, and core threat classifications.
2. **IBM Plex Sans (User Interface & Body):** Engineered for maximum legibility in dense multi-column interfaces. Provides structural neutrality for UI interaction, filters, form fields, and analysis prose.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Data / Metrics / Timestamps / IDs):** Unambiguous tabular figures and fixed-pitch alignment for ISO-8601 timestamps, account UID hashes, Telegram channel handles, message velocity rates, and geospatial coordinates.

### Typographic Rules
- All uppercase section indicators and taxonomy pills render in `label-sm` with `0.06em` letter spacing.
- Raw metric readouts, delta percentages, and coordinate matrices must strictly enforce tabular numerals (`tnum`).
- Punctuation inside threat summaries remains proportional; identifiers and hashes inside log streams must never wrap across lines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an enterprise operational instrument panel: dense, modular, persistent, and fluid. The screen is treated not as a page, but as a dynamic grid of docked monitors and stream inspectors.

### Layout Model
- **Master Grid:** An edge-to-edge multi-pane system consisting of a persistent 56px global utility spine, a 280px-360px collapsible workspace filter dock, a flexible central canvas (multi-column streams or node-link graph views), and an optional 380px contextual forensic inspector.
- **Rhythm:** Built strictly on an internal 4px baseline sub-grid (`0.25rem` intervals). Component padding favors horizontal expansion over vertical bloat to maximize data rows visible above the fold.
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1440px):** Multi-pane simultaneous triage (Channels + Active Narrative + Forensic Inspector).
  - **Compact Desktop / Tablet Landscape (1024px - 1439px):** Inspector transitions into a sliding overlay drawer; stream views collapse from three columns to two.
  - **Field Screen / Tablet Portrait (< 1024px):** Single-pane sequential views with persistent bottom instrument switcher; filters move to a full-screen sheet.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual order is articulated strictly through **Tonal Stacking and Hairline Inset Framing**. Soft blurs, drop shadows, and glassmorphic transparencies are forbidden.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#0A0E14` — Base background substrate for unselected voids and structural troughs.
2. **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Grid Panes):** `#12161F` framed by continuous 1px `#232838` borders. These represent persistent functional panels (e.g., Telegram channel firehose, sentiment timeline).
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Items & Cards):** `#171C27` applied to hovered list rows, active log records, and nested payload blocks within a pane.
4. **Level 3 (Modals & Command Palettes):** `#171C27` with a 1px solid `#3E465D` boundary stroke and a crisp, zero-blur perimeter offset (e.g., `0 0 0 1px #232838, 0 12px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`). The shadow functions purely to separate overlapping layers, never as aesthetic decoration.

### Spatial Boundaries
Adjacent panels do not use empty space for isolation; they abut via shared single-pixel hairline dividers (`#232838`). Contrast between interactive zones and non-interactive zones is conveyed by background value stepping rather than volumetric elevation.

## Shapes

The interface adheres to an engineered **0.375rem (6px)** radius profile (`roundedness: 1`), conveying a machined, industrial-grade software instrument.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Containers & Tiles:** Standard 6px (`0.375rem`) corner radius applied to all panels, cards, message bubbles, and search fields.
- **Tags & Status Badges:** 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius for metric pills and categorical tags. Fully pill-shaped geometries (9999px) are strictly prohibited, as roundedness must suggest structured technical tags rather than consumer-oriented buttons.
- **Micro Elements:** Checkbox bounds, color swatches, and small state flags use a sharp 2px radius or absolute square edges (0px) to preserve precise hairline rendering on standard-DPI monitor arrays.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid flat `#E8823A` background with `#0A0E14` bold typography. Hover shifts brightness to `#F09454`. No shadow, no gradient.
- **Secondary / Panel Action:** `#171C27` background, 1px `#232838` border, `#EDECE6` text. Hover updates border to `#8B92A5`.
- **Destructive Action:** 1px solid `#D9564F` hairline with a transparent or 10% `#D9564F` background fill; text in `#D9564F`.
- **Sizing:** Compact `32px` standard operational height, `24px` micro for inline stream triggers; padding `0 12px` (`space-md`).

### Data Chips & Risk Badges
- Non-pill structure (4px corner radius). Composed of a 1px border matching the semantic color at 30% alpha, a deep 12% tint background fill, and high-legibility semantic text.
- Formats:
  - High Risk: `#D9564F` text on `rgba(217, 86, 79, 0.12)` fill, `1px solid rgba(217, 86, 79, 0.3)`.
  - Medium Risk: `#D9A441` text on `rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.12)` fill, `1px solid rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.3)`.
  - Verified / Authentic: `#3EAF7C` text on `rgba(62, 175, 124, 0.12)` fill, `1px solid rgba(62, 175, 124, 0.3)`.
  - Network Indicator: `#5FA1C4` text on `rgba(95, 161, 196, 0.12)` fill.

### Narrative & Message Cards
- Encased in `#12161F` with a 1px `#232838` border and 6px radius.
- Header row contains target handle or channel name in `IBM Plex Sans` (Semibold), flanked by UTC timestamp (`JetBrains Mono` in `#8B92A5`) and a risk status chip.
- Content zone renders raw narrative text with highlighted entities (amber outline on text click).
- Footer houses metadata telemetry metrics (reposts, quote-velocity, forwarding depth) displayed with monospace figures.

### Form Inputs & Terminal Filters
- Height `32px`, flat `#0A0E14` base fill, 1px `#232838` hairline perimeter.
- Inactive placeholder text set to `#565C6B`.
- Focused state replaces the default border with 1px solid `#E8823A` without exterior bloom or focus glow rings.
- Query filters support mono-styled syntax keys (e.g., `lang:fa`, `ch:tg_channel`, `risk:>80`).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- **Checkboxes:** 14px square boxes, 1px `#232838` border. When active, solid `#E8823A` fill with `#0A0E14` check icon.
- **Segmented Controls:** Enclosed container `#0A0E14` with a 1px border. Child options sit flush; selected segment activates `#171C27` background, 1px `#232838` edge, and crisp `#EDECE6` text.

### Log Streams & Chronology Lists
- Dense row height (`28px` to `36px`), alternating striping avoided in favor of 1px border-bottom (`#232838`) lines.
- Hover produces an immediate, un-transitioned step to `#171C27`. Selected log item receives a 2px left border accent in `#E8823A`.
